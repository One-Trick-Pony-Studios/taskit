# taskit

App to create any selected text into a task in your calendar app.

TaskIt: Turn Any Text into a Calendar Task

TaskIt lets you create calendar events from any app with just a tap. Select or copy text from messages, emails, or notes — then share it to TaskIt. Instantly, your words become a task, ready to be scheduled in the calendar app of your choice.


⚡ One-Tap Flow: No typing, no clutter — just tap and confirm 
🔒 Private & Offline: Works entirely on-device, no internet required 
📅 Calendar Freedom: Launches your preferred calendar app for final edits and save
📋 Full Context: Uses your original text as the event title and description 
🗓️ Smart Parsing: Automatically detects dates and times from natural language 

Whether it’s a reminder, a meeting, or a quick to-do, TaskIt turns intent into action — fast.
